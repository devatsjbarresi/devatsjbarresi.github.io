ShowShark 1.0.BETA5 is out, along with ShowSquid.
ShowShark 1.0.BETA5

Hello,

I thought you might be interested in a quick update about ShowShark, the Wireshark plugin for understanding entertainment network traffic. Version 1.0.BETA5 is now available, and it is the release candidate for version 1.0.
Visit the ShowShark Website

What’s New in BETA5?

  • mDNS and OSC support.
  • DMX and OSC Watchers for tracking values across a capture.
  • Host Table improvements, including filters, CSV export and First Seen and Last Seen columns.
  • Better Filter Builder behaviour.
  • Improved menus and customisation.
  • General fixes and performance improvements.

Introducing ShowSquid

ShowSquid is a desktop application for Windows and macOS that works alongside ShowShark. It prompts quiet devices to respond and listens for multicast traffic that might not otherwise reach your computer, helping ShowShark discover more of what’s happening on the network.
