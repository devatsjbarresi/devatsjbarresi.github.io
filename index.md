<h1 align="center">Welcome to ShowShark</h1>

---

ShowShark is a Wireshark plugin designed for entertainment industry professionals of all experience levels, including those who are learning or exploring entertainment networks for the first time.

Its goal is to make entertainment networking easier to understand, while also providing practical tools for fault-finding and visualising what is happening on your network.

Whether you are an experienced professional or just starting out, ShowShark helps you see what is really happening between devices on your network.
